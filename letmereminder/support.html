Frequently asked questions

I don’t get the reminders.
In iPhone Settings, Notifications, LetMeRemind, check that notifications are allowed.
I’m changing iPhones.
If you restore the new iPhone from a backup of the old one, your data comes along; turn the lock of the app back on in its Settings, because its code doesn’t leave the old iPhone. Otherwise, in LetMeRemind, Settings, Data, “Export an encrypted file,” then, on the new iPhone, “Import a file,” with the same password.
A notification shows the name or the amount.
In the reminder, turn on “Private”: the notification then only says “You have a reminder.” Treatments and health reminders are private from the moment they are created. The iOS setting “Show Previews” (iPhone Settings, Notifications) also hides the content while the iPhone is locked.
I don’t want anyone to act from the Lock Screen.
In LetMeRemind, Settings, Security, turn on “Unlock to act from a notification”: “Paid,” “Done,” “Checked,” and “Snooze” will first ask you to unlock the iPhone.
I forgot the code of the app.
Face ID opens the app without the code, unless the faces enrolled in Face ID have changed: the app then asks for its code once. Without Face ID, the code can’t be recovered: there is no back door. That’s why a regular encrypted export serves as a backup: with it, you can reinstall the app and import everything again.
During the beta: how do I report a problem?
Take a screenshot in the app: TestFlight offers to send it with a comment, which reaches the developer through Apple. After a crash, TestFlight also offers to send a comment.

No account, no data with the developer

LetMeRemind has no account and no server: the developer holds none of your data, which stays on your iPhone and in its backups. That is also why a lost export password or a forgotten code of the app can’t be recovered. Details in the privacy policy.
