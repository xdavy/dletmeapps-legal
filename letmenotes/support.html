MeNotes support

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

Write to the developer

MeNotes is developed by Davy Ushaka Ishimwe. Write to aadlm.ergonomic338@passmail.net.

Please include in your message:

  • your iPhone model;
  • your iOS version (Settings › General › About);
  • your MeNotes version, shown at the bottom of the MeNotes settings, for example “MeNotes 1.0 (6)”;
  • if the MeNotes recovery screen offers “Copier le détail” (copy the details), the copied text: it only contains error codes, no notes.

Do not send your notes or your passwords: they are not needed to help you.

No account, no data held by the developer

MeNotes has no account and no server. Your notes stay on your iPhone and the developer has no copy of them: they can neither recover them nor reset a password or a passcode. Your best insurance is an exported backup, kept in a safe place, together with its password.

MeNotes runs on iPhone, with iOS 26 or later.

Frequently asked questions

MeNotes shows “Clé de chiffrement indisponible” (encryption key unavailable)
Your notes are encrypted with a key kept in iCloud Keychain. If you have just changed iPhone or turned the keychain back on, wait a few minutes for it to sync, then tap “Réessayer” (try again). If the key still cannot be found, “Repartir de zéro” (start over) opens empty data without erasing the old data; then restore your latest exported backup. Without the key and without a backup, nobody can read your notes, not even the developer.
I am changing iPhone
The safe way: on the old iPhone, export an encrypted backup (MeNotes settings › Sauvegarde & restauration › Exporter) and keep it in Files or iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, install MeNotes, then tap “Restaurer…” (restore) and enter the backup password. Your name, your profile photo and the app passcode are not in the backup: set them up again. If you transfer your whole iPhone instead, MeNotes looks for its key in iCloud Keychain; if “Clé de chiffrement indisponible” appears, wait for the sync, then tap “Réessayer”.
Backing up and restoring
In the MeNotes settings, section “Sauvegarde & restauration” (backup and restore): “Exporter” (export) creates a file, encrypted by default with a password of at least 8 characters; “Restaurer…” (restore) reads a file and asks for its password if it is encrypted. Restoring replaces all the current content of MeNotes with the content of the backup. Upcoming reminders are rescheduled if notifications are allowed. Keep the password: without it the backup cannot be read, and the developer cannot recover it.
I cannot open a locked note
A note is protected either by Face ID or by a password of at least 6 characters. A note protected by Face ID asks for Face ID while “Secure ID · Face ID” is on in the MeNotes settings; if Face ID is denied to MeNotes in the iPhone settings, turn Secure ID off to open it. A note protected by a password always asks for it: MeNotes only keeps a fingerprint of it and cannot recover it if you forget it. A protected note has no tags and stays out of search results until it has been opened.
A reminder does not appear
Check that notifications are allowed for MeNotes (Settings › Notifications › MeNotes). A reminder MeNotes could not schedule is flagged in red in the task: edit it to try again.
