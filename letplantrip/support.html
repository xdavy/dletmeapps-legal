Frequently asked questions

How do backups work, and why keep my recovery phrase?

In Account, “Back up now” makes a file holding all your trips, encrypted with your recovery phrase. The app makes the phrase at your first backup and shows it to you: write it down and keep it away from your iPhone. On this iPhone, Face ID or your passcode is enough to open your backups; on another iPhone, you will need the phrase. If you change it, earlier backups still open on this iPhone, and elsewhere with the phrase of their time. Nobody can recover a lost phrase, the developer included.

How do I move my trips to a new iPhone?

Make a backup on the old iPhone, then bring the file to the new one: through the copy in iCloud Drive (the “letplantrip” folder in the Files app), or by sharing the backup from “Backups on this iPhone”. On the new iPhone, open Account, choose “Restore from a file”, pick the backup and type your recovery phrase. Restoring adds the trips; it never deletes those already there.

What does “Copy to iCloud Drive” do?

When it is on, each backup, already encrypted, is also copied to the letplantrip folder of your iCloud Drive, which keeps the 10 most recent of this iPhone. It is off until you turn it on in Account.

Can the app remind me to back up?

Yes: choose a frequency in Account, under “Regular backup”. The app never backs up on its own; when your last backup is older than the frequency you chose, it reminds you at 7 PM.

How do I lock the app?

In Account, turn on “Lock with Face ID”. The app then opens locked, and locks again after 5 minutes away from it; Face ID or your passcode opens it.

How do I add a place from Google Maps?

In Google Maps, share the place and choose letplantrip in the share sheet. Pick the trip, the stop and the category, then “Add”: the place appears in the Places tab the next time you open the app.

How do I use the app in English or in French?

The app follows your iPhone's language, and shows English for any language other than French. To give letplantrip a language of its own, go to Settings → Apps → letplantrip → Language. iOS only shows this line when your iPhone has at least two languages in Settings → General → Language & Region.

How do I delete my data?

Delete trips or backups in the app, or delete the app. Copies in iCloud Drive are deleted from the app or in the Files app. The recovery phrase stays in this iPhone's keychain after the app is deleted; the privacy policy explains why.

Does the app send my data anywhere?

Your trips leave your iPhone only in the backups you make and the iCloud Drive copies you turn on, both encrypted. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no developer server. The app asks Apple for map backgrounds and positions, and Google for where a shared link leads. Details are in the privacy policy.
